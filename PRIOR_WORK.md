# Prior work, source reuse and related submissions

## Mathematical sources

The vertex-splitting construction appears in He, Ma and Yang's 2019 preprint,
Section 10. The construction is credited to that source in the manuscript.
[Original preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1912.00986v1)

Ning and Zhai's 2021 preprint reports an announcement by He, Ma and Yang of a
stronger conclusion at n = q^2 + q + 2 for large powers of four. The source
attributes it to a 2021 talk. The available private literature review did not
locate a complete written proof behind that announcement. The 2025 published
version omits the announcement. Its priority implications remain unresolved.
This project makes no claim to have established first-discovery priority.
[2021 preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.15279v1)

The mathematical exposition cites the stability work of He, Ma and Yang.
[Longer paper](https://arxiv.org/html/1912.00986v4)
